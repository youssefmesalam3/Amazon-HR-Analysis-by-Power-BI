# 📊 Amazon HR Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon HR Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI** dashboard designed to analyze employee data and provide insights into workforce performance, satisfaction, employee turnover, salary levels, promotions, projects, and work accidents.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management understand employee trends and identify factors that may impact employee retention and overall workforce performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the overall employee workforce.
* Monitor employee turnover and **Employee Left Rate**.
* Analyze employee satisfaction across different departments and salary levels.
* Compare employees based on salary categories.
* Analyze the number of projects handled by each department.
* Evaluate employee promotions over the last five years.
* Analyze work accidents and employee turnover by department.
* Explore the relationship between promotions and the number of projects.
* Support HR decision-making through interactive data visualization.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 👥 Employee & Department Analysis

The dashboard analyzes the number of projects across different departments, including:

* Sales
* Technical
* Support
* IT
* R&D
* Product Management
* Marketing
* Accounting
* HR
* Management

Sales has the highest number of projects, followed by Technical and Support departments.

### 💰 Salary Analysis

Employees are categorized into three salary levels:

* Low
* Medium
* High

The dashboard provides insights into employee distribution across salary categories and analyzes employee turnover by salary level.

### 😊 Employee Satisfaction

The dashboard analyzes the **Average Satisfaction** across departments and salary levels, allowing management to identify differences in employee satisfaction between different workforce segments.

### 📈 Promotion Analysis

The dashboard tracks the **number of promotions during the last five years** across departments and compares promotion levels with the number of projects.

### ⚠️ Work Accident & Employee Turnover Analysis

The dashboard compares **work accidents and employees who left** across departments, helping identify departments with higher workforce risks or turnover levels.

### 🔄 Promotion vs. Projects

A dedicated visualization analyzes the relationship between:

* Number of Projects
* Promotions in the Last 5 Years

This helps evaluate whether departments with higher project workloads also have higher promotion levels.

### 💼 Employee Turnover by Salary

The dashboard also analyzes employees who left the company according to salary level, providing insights into the relationship between compensation and employee retention.
